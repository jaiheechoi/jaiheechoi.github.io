We study whether the change in absolute lymphocyte count after conditioning chemotherapy (the delta lymphocyte index) reflects its pharmacodynamic effect in large B-cell lymphoma patients receiving CAR T-cell therapy, finding that it is independently associated with survival and linked to germline variants related to drug metabolism and macrophage biology.
