Using UK Biobank and Million Veteran Program data, we apply empirical Bayes composite null testing to identify truly pleiotropic variants for pancreatic cancer and diabetes, then show that polygenic risk scores built from these variants predict pancreatic cancer risk better than scores built from single-disease findings.
