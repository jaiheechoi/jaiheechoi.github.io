We study how seemingly minor differences in genetic data processing pipelines affect set-based hypothesis testing and effect size estimation in GWAS, framing the problem as model misspecification. We derive the resulting differences in power and bias analytically, validate them in simulations, and illustrate them in a pancreatic cancer sequencing study, where a known association with ATM can disappear depending on processing choices.
