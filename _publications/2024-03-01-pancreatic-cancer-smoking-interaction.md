Using genome-wide data from the UK Biobank with robust resampling corrections for interaction testing, we identify a variant on chromosome 2 whose effect on pancreatic cancer risk is modified by smoking, replicate it in independent cohorts, and show that polygenic risk scores including interaction effects discriminate better than main-effects-only scores.
