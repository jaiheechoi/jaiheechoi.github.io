We propose two set-based inference procedures for interval-censored outcomes with competing risks: a variance-components test (crSKAT) suited to heterogeneous variant effects and a burden test (crBurden) suited to homogeneous effects. Both control type I error and improve power over ad hoc alternatives, and we apply them to search for genes associated with fracture risk in the UK Biobank, treating death as a competing event.
