Large biobanks often record disease onset as interval-censored data, which is frequently collapsed to binary outcomes at the cost of information. We develop methodology to test genetic variant sets, such as genes or pathways, against multiple interval-censored outcomes simultaneously, aggregating information across correlated phenotypes to gain power. We apply the approach to identify genes affecting risk of bone fractures and falls in the UK Biobank.
