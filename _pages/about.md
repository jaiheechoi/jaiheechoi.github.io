I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences at Marquette University. My research focuses on developing robust, scalable, and interpretable statistical methodology for extracting meaningful insight from complex, high-dimensional biological data. Much of my work lies at the intersection of statistical genetics, survival analysis, and Bayesian modeling, with the goal of better understanding the genetic architecture underlying complex traits.

I received my PhD in Statistics from Rice University under the supervision of Dr. Ryan Sun in the Department of Biostatistics at The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center.

Outside of research, I enjoy running, reading, rock climbing, and going on walks my dog, Marvin.
